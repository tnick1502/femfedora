Note: the SWAN median (52.5) is about 2.5 y later than the InterLACE median (50.0). SWAN has an observed-FMP, prospective design with left truncation (women entered at age 42–52 still menstruating). ## 2. STRAW+10 stage timing relative to FMP

Definitions used in SWAN/ReSTAGE (Paramsothy 2017, URL above):
- Early MT: a persistent difference of ≥7 days in the length of consecutive cycles, recurring within 10 cycles of the first variable cycle.
- Late MT: the first cycle ≥60 days.
- FMP: a bleed followed by ≥12 months of amenorrhea.
- Cycle: a bleed followed by ≥3 bleed-free days.
